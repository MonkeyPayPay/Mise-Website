The back-of-house suite

Seventeen departments. One login. One truth.

This is the tour — every section of the Mise back office, with the actual screens. All of it reads and writes the same data the POS and the book do, so nothing here is an export of anything else.

Walk it yourself in the live demo See what’s in each tier

POS management

Studio, menus & the device fleet

Configure the register without a service call: POS Studio lays out menus, modifiers and screens; devices enroll into a managed fleet; the 86 board and order queue run the shift.

  • Menu & modifier management with Studio layout control
  • Terminal fleet enrollment, roles and PIN switch
  • Live order queue with kitchen pacing
  • 86 board wired to inventory

Marketing

Segments, campaigns & journeys

Audiences build themselves from the guest book — visits, spend, tags. Campaigns and automated journeys send over email with attribution you can defend.

  • Self-building segments from real guest data
  • Email campaigns & automated journeys
  • Attribution tied to actual visits and spend
  • Consent and complaint-rate guardrails built in

Inventory

Live depletion & menu engineering

Every sale depletes its recipe at moving-average cost. Counts, waste and variance keep it honest; menu engineering plots every dish by margin and popularity.

  • Real-time depletion from POS sales
  • Recipe & plate costing, sub-recipes included
  • Waste logs, physical counts, theoretical-vs-actual variance
  • Auto-86 at zero with kitchen notification

Scheduling

Labor that watches the P&L

Draft, publish, swap with approvals — measured against live labor % and an event-aware forecast, with overtime flagged before it’s worked.

  • Draft → publish with app notifications
  • Swap marketplace with conflict & OT checks
  • Time clock with break attestation
  • Labor % vs live sales, event-aware forecasting

Procurement

Vendors, POs & receiving

Vendor catalogs with pack sizes, suggested orders from live stock against par, receiving that updates cost and inventory and drafts the invoice — with price-drift alerts.

  • Vendor catalogs & pack-size math
  • Suggested POs from par and on-hand
  • Receiving updates moving-average cost instantly
  • Price-drift alerts before they eat the margin

Financial

The live P&L

Prime cost computed from invoices and punches, a daily flash, a rolling P&L, GL-categorized AP and budget-vs-actual — current to the last closed check.

  • Computed prime cost, continuously
  • Daily flash & rolling 30-day P&L
  • GL-categorized accounts payable
  • Budget vs actual on true business dates
  • CSV always; GL/QBO export

Reports

Ask the data anything

Heatmaps, server performance, drill-down chains and a report builder over a canonical read API — the same numbers every module already agrees on.

  • Report builder with saved views
  • Sales heatmaps & server performance
  • Drill chains from summary to source rows
  • Canonical read API on Business+

Portfolio

Multi-location, rolled up

See the group at a glance, then drill into any venue. Group-level config, cross-venue transfers and allocation — one operating picture for the whole portfolio.

  • Portfolio rollups across venues
  • Group config with local overrides
  • Cross-venue transfers & allocation
  • Same-basis comparisons, no spreadsheet stitching

Gift & AR

Gift cards & house accounts

Sell and redeem gift cards across the group; run house accounts with statements for the regulars and corporate clients who expect them.

  • Gift card sale, redemption & liability tracking
  • House accounts with credit limits
  • Statements & AR aging
  • Works across locations on Business+

Checklists

HACCP & opening/closing discipline

Opening, closing and HACCP logs with captured values — temps, ppm — and a completion audit trail. Compliance becomes a record, not a binder.

  • Opening/closing templates per daypart
  • HACCP logs with captured readings
  • Photo evidence and sign-off
  • Completion audit trail

Team

Comms where work happens

Channels, a manager logbook, read receipts — and automated posts from the system itself: 86s, schedule publishes, price alerts, terminal outages.

  • Channels & DMs with read receipts
  • Manager logbook that survives turnover
  • Automated system posts (86s, publishes, alerts)
  • Tasks tied to real work

Audit

Every action, attributed

Voids, comps, price changes, config publishes, support actions — dated, attributed, revertible. MFA on sensitive roles; device-level control on terminals.

  • Attributed, revertible config revisions
  • Void/comp/price-change trails
  • Support-console actions ledgered to the venue
  • MFA & managed devices

Integrations

Adapters & the open edge

POS adapters for Toast, Square and Lightspeed; Stripe, Twilio and SendGrid on the rails; SSO/OIDC for identity; webhooks and a canonical API on Business+.

  • POS adapter connections (direct & aggregator)
  • Stripe payments · Twilio SMS · SendGrid email
  • OIDC single sign-on
  • Outbound webhooks + canonical API (Business+)

Settings

Config you can trust

Every setting is a dated, attributed revision with rollback — publish a change, see who changed what, revert cleanly. Plus a command palette that gets you anywhere fast.

  • Versioned config with rollback
  • Role-scoped admin surfaces
  • Command palette navigation
  • Per-venue and group-level settings

Events & BEO

Private dining as a profit center

A lead pipeline from inquiry to booked, proposals with e-signature, the BEO and production sheet, payment schedules with deposits — and a true event P&L, because the costs already live here.

  • Pipeline: inquiry → proposal → contract → booked
  • BEO + kitchen production sheet, printable
  • Payment schedules, deposits, client portal
  • True event P&L per booking

Tip payouts

From pool math to money moved

The pool calculation engine handles roles, hours and points; payout runs turn the math into money — including an instant rail — with the compliance trail attached.

  • Pool calc by role, hours or points
  • Payout runs with approval flow
  • Instant payout rail (flat per-payout fee, never a % of tips)
  • Compliance packet & audit trail

Ops boards

The shift, on one wall

Pre-shift briefings and operational boards that pull from the live system — counts, 86s, VIPs tonight, tasks — so the huddle runs on facts, not memory.

  • Pre-shift report generated from live data
  • Boards for tasks, counts and priorities
  • VIP and allergy callouts for tonight
  • Updates as the system updates
