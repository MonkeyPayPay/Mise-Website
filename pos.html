Mise POS · terminal software

A terminal built for fine dining, the bar, and the counter

Order entry that keeps up with a slammed service: coursing, bar tabs with card pre-auth, split and merge without drama — and when the internet drops, service doesn’t. Every closed check moves inventory, labor and the P&L in the same second.

What’s inside

Service modes for how you actually serve

Coursed order entry

Fine-dining mode courses the ticket — fire apps, hold mains, and the kitchen sees the sequence you intend, not a wall of items.

Bar tabs with pre-auth

Open a tab against a card pre-authorization, keep pouring, close without chasing a walkout. Quick-service mode rings and turns just as fast.

Split & merge, sanely

Split by seat, by item or evenly; merge parties that combined — the check history stays auditable through every rearrangement.

Offline-resilient by design

Terminals queue every action in an offline outbox and replay it idempotently when the link returns — the same order never posts twice, and the back office is told how long you were dark.

KDS & chit routing

Tickets route to the right kitchen display or printer by station; expo sees the whole picture, the sauté station sees sauté.

Drawers & cash discipline

Drawer sessions, paid-ins/outs, blind counts and over/short land in the cash ledger — the count is a record, not a sticky note.

Payments & devices

Cards through Stripe, a fleet you can manage

Card payments, deposits and card-on-file run through Stripe — Mise stores tokens, never card numbers. Terminals are enrolled devices in a managed fleet: pre-enrolled, permissioned by role, and revocable from the back office.

  • Tokenized cards; deposits and card-on-file for events and reservations
  • Per-device enrollment with role-scoped staff PIN switch
  • Customer-facing display support for totals and tips

One source of truth

Close a check. The restaurant updates.

Sales hit the flash, recipes deplete inventory, the 86 board fires, the guest’s profile grows, labor % recalculates — one closed check, every module, the same second. That’s the point of running the POS inside the operating system.
