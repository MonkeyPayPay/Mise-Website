Pricing

Per location. Never per cover.

Two families — keep your POS, or run ours — four tiers each, and a build-your-own package priced by the same engine. No per-cover fees, no % of your revenue, no integrity features held ransom by tier.

Back-office: the full suite minus our terminal — connect the POS you already run (Toast, Square, Lightspeed).

Annual pricing shows the monthly-equivalent and the total billed today — always both. Usage (email/SMS/instant-payout overages) bills monthly in arrears and is never discounted or prepaid. Prices are launch defaults from the platform price book.
